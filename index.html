About saro

Our Mission

Our mission at saro is to allow beginner budgeters to control their finances like a professional.


Our Story

saro was created by Alex Strickland. He struggled to use other money budgeting apps, so he created something that was personal and unique for himself. Once he realized the idea could be suitable for others, he began expanding his operations and creating new features for others to enjoy.

Our Values

  • Strength: We have packed this software full of features.
  • Innovation: We are always striving to improve our software.
  • Creativity: Our stuff is groundbreaking.
  • Innovation: Have we said innovation? Our software is the only one on the market with all our features.

Powerful Features

Smart Budget Creation

saro analyzes your income and spending patterns to suggest personalized budgets that are realistic and achievable. Our AI-powered system adapts to your lifestyle and financial goals.

Expense Tracking

Automatically categorize and track your expenses across multiple accounts. Connect your bank accounts, credit cards, and other financial services for a complete overview of your spending.

Financial Insights

Gain valuable insights into your spending habits with detailed analytics and visualizations. Identify areas where you can cut costs and optimize your budget.

Goal Setting

Set financial goals like saving for a vacation, paying off debt, or building an emergency fund. saro helps you track your progress and stay motivated.

Bill Reminders

Never miss a payment with automated bill reminders. Schedule recurring expenses and receive notifications before due dates.
